# Astra NX-200 Warranty and Support SLA Policy

## Standard Hardware Warranty

All Astra NX-200 Industrial Edge Gateway units (`AST-NX200-R2`) include a standard **36-month** (3-year) return-to-depot hardware warranty starting from the original invoice date.

Customers may purchase the optional **AstraCare Plus** support package within 90 days of hardware purchase. AstraCare Plus extends total hardware warranty coverage to **60 months** (5 years) and upgrades hardware replacement from standard depot repair to next-business-day advance unit replacement.

## Warranty Exclusions

Hardware warranty coverage does not apply under any of the following conditions:

- The internal chassis tamper seal (`TS-04`) is broken or removed for any modification other than authorized CR2032W RTC battery replacement, M.2 NVMe SSD installation, or `AST-TPAD-15` thermal interface pad maintenance performed according to the official SOP.
- Liquid ingress, condensation, or conductive dust exposure exceeding the enclosure's **IP40** ingress protection rating.
- Connection to unapproved DC power sources exceeding **52 V DC** continuous input, or lightning/surge transients on unshielded RS-485 fieldbus lines without external surge arrestors.

## RMA and Escalation SLA

Enterprise support requests are prioritized according to operational severity:

- **Severity P1 (Production Line Down):** Initial engineering response within **2 hours** (24x7x365). For units covered under AstraCare Plus, an advance replacement unit ships within **1 business day**.
- **Severity P2 (Degraded Redundancy / Fieldbus Fault):** Initial engineering response within **6 business hours**, with root-cause diagnostic turnaround within 2 business days.
- **Severity P3 (General Configuration & Firmware Inquiry):** Initial response within **1 business day**.

Every hardware return requires an authorized Return Merchandise Authorization number formatted as `RMA-NX-YYYY-NNNN`. When an advance replacement unit is delivered, the defective unit must be shipped back to the regional depot within **14 calendar days** of replacement delivery to avoid a full hardware non-return invoice.
